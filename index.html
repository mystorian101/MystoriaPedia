About this guide

A few notes on how the guide is organised, for anyone editing it later.

Anomaly is kept separate from the 22-type matchup chart — treat it as a rare / secret classification (event creatures, corrupted forms, etc.) rather than a type with standard weaknesses and resistances, unless you decide otherwise.

Numbering on the type chart (163–184) is carried over from the internal type-list spreadsheet so it's easy to cross-reference against the original source. Species and move numbers are placeholders — renumber freely as the dex fills in.

Capturing

The loop that gets a wild creature into your roster.

  1. Encounter — a wild creature appears, usually tied to a zone and a set of possible species.
  2. Weaken — battle it down, or apply a status effect, to improve capture odds.
  3. Throw — use a capture item; success scales with remaining health, status, and item tier.
  4. Confirm — a captured creature joins your roster or storage, keeping its individual stats.
Leave room here for capture-item tiers (e.g. Common / Greater / Mystic Orb) once those are designed.
